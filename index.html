FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start.

How much does a typical project cost? ▼

Project costs vary greatly depending on the service. Security fixes and patches range from $200–$500. Standard store setups, marketing flows, and redesigns start at $800. Full custom development and complex API integrations range from $5,000 to $25,000.

Do you offer payment plans? ▼

Yes — we require 50% upfront and 50% upon launch. Milestone payments are available for larger projects.

How long does it take to see results? ▼

You will see measurable improvements within 30–60 days post-launch. SEO and organic traffic build over 3–6 months.

Do you work with existing stores? ▼

Absolutely. We offer both brand new store builds and optimization/redesigns for existing Shopify stores.
